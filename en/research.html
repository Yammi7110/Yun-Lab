RESEARCH AREAS

Research

Current and historical research topics of the laboratory.

01

Magnetic Characterization and Excitation Waveform Control

Measurement of B–H characteristics and iron loss in electrical steels under sinusoidal, PWM inverter, six-step, and arbitrary waveform excitation. Development of waveform control methods, single-sheet testing, and improved measurement systems.

02

DC-Biased and Two-Dimensional Magnetic Properties

Measurement and analysis of electrical steel magnetic properties under DC-biased magnetization and two-dimensional magnetic excitation.

03

Soft Magnetic Powders and Powder Cores

Investigation of gas- and water-atomized powders, insulating coatings, particle size, compaction, annealing, permeability, hysteresis loss, and eddy-current loss. Development of binder-free powder cores.

04

High-Efficiency Motors and Magnetic Cores

Study of magnetization under motor operating conditions, comparison of laminated and powder cores, in-situ magnetization measurements in induction motors, and reduction of motor-core losses.

05

Magnetic Shielding

Magnetic flux guidance and reduction of external magnetic fields using soft magnetic materials; evaluation of shielding structures and performance.

06

Electromagnetic Shielding

Study of electromagnetic reflection, multiple reflection, attenuation, eddy-current loss, and shielding effectiveness, including lock-in-amplifier-based measurements.

07

Electromagnetic Field Analysis and Simulation

Electromagnetic field simulations and analysis of magnetic flux distributions in magnetic materials, shielding systems, and motors.

08

Dissimilar Metal Joining

Joining of iron–aluminum and titanium–aluminum systems, resistance spot welding, and improvements in joint strength using interlayers.

09

Low-Temperature Synthesis of Aluminum Nitride

A historical research topic from the former laboratory website. Detailed results have not yet been verified.

10

Variable Permanent Magnets and Magnetic Field Design

Variable-field configurations based on permanent magnets, including Halbach arrays, with potential applications in accelerators and plasma control.

11

Inverter–Motor–Battery System Optimization

Reduction of core loss and improvement of drive-system efficiency through magnetic flux density waveform control and excitation condition optimization.
