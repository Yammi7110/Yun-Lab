Principal Investigator

Kyyoul YUN

Otemon Gakuin University

Education

  • Ph.D. in Energy Science, Kyoto University (2009)
  • M.S. in Metallurgical Engineering, Kyungpook National University (2005)
  • B.S. in Metallurgical Engineering, Kyungpook National University (2003)

Academic Career

Research and teaching appointments at Shinshu University, Toyota Technological Institute, and Gifu University, followed by an appointment at Otemon Gakuin University.

Research Interests

Soft magnetic materials, magnetic property measurement, magnetic applications, dissimilar metal joining, and variable permanent magnet systems.

Professional Societies

Historical profile: IEEE, The Institute of Electrical Engineers of Japan, and the Japan Society of Applied Electromagnetics and Mechanics. Current memberships are subject to confirmation.
